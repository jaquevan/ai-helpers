# Service blueprint

Start from `canvas.json` in this folder. A blueprint is the journey plus what the organization does underneath it. Phases are columns, left to right. Lanes are rows and stay aligned across columns.

## How to fill it

- **Customer actions** are what the person does, same standard as a journey map's action row.
- **Frontstage** is what the person can see: the interface, the employee, the email.
- **Backstage** is the work that supports that moment and is invisible to the person.
- **Support** is systems, partners, and policy behind the backstage work.
- **Evidence** is what the moment leaves behind: a screen, a ticket, a message, a receipt.
- Rename Before, During, and After to the real stages. Add or remove a whole column, and keep all five lanes.
- The gap between frontstage and backstage is the line the customer cannot see. Do not mix those two lanes.
- Build this after the journey is known, or alongside it. Do not hide the customer's actions in order to show internal process.
- Replace every placeholder. Label assumptions.
